# Public video descriptions

## They Loved Him More When He Was Useful

Everybody called Eli a lifesaver. His daughter just wanted him in the front row.
A quick favor becomes another favor, then the evening he promised someone else.
When a child's empty chair costs more than a room full of applause, Eli has to
find out who misses him—and who only misses what he does for them.

An original fictional animated short about approval, people-pleasing and the
price of always saying yes. Watch the hands, the ticket, the star, and the seat.

## What If Every Lie Left a Mark on Your Face?

Noah wished liars were easier to spot. One morning, they were.
A crack on a cheek exposes the friend who betrayed him. Confessing erases it.
At first, the world finally feels fair. Then a cruel truth hurts a child, a loving
lie marks his mother, and Noah's own clean face stops looking so innocent.

An original fictional thought experiment: would visible lies make us kinder,
or just give us a new way to judge each other?
