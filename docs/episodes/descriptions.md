# Public video descriptions

These descriptions are distinct from the longer workflow creative briefs.

## Psychology

**Your Brain Isn't Lazy—Your Goals Might Be the Problem**

What if the goal making you feel like a failure was never really yours? Follow Milo
as a wall of perfect ambitions turns into a very heavy backpack—and one uncomfortable
question changes what he does next. A warm animated story about borrowed expectations,
meaningful goals, and making the first step small enough to take.

The characters are fictional. The discussion draws on research into self-concordant
goals and implementation intentions; sources accompany the script. This is an
educational story, not a diagnosis or a promise that motivation fixes real constraints.

## What-if scenarios

**What If the Internet Disappeared for 30 Days? Cash Isn't Enough**

The lights stay on. The phone is charged. The next delivery has vanished from the app.
In this fictional thirty-day outage, Ada and Milo discover that buying things is only
one part of keeping a city running. Follow paper routes, missing orders, rumors,
community radio, and the connections that a stack of cash cannot replace.

A hypothetical scenario, not a forecast: public Internet data cannot route, while
power, local storage and radio remain. Telephone availability varies by system.
The people, timeline, and social consequences are invented.
